English slogan: **Quality under control.**

Brand architecture:
- Q — Quality
- A — Audit
- R — Risk
- I — Incident
- C — CAPA
- A — Action / Improvement

Public-facing branding must remain organization-neutral. Legacy technical identifiers may be retained when changing them would risk compatibility with database, authorization, integrations, or historical records.
